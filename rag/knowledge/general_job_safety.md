# General Job Search Safety

## Verification

Before accepting a suspicious job offer, verify:

- The employer's identity
- The company's official website
- The recruiter's identity
- The job description
- Contact information
- Any payment or financial requests

## Warning Signs

Potential warning signs include:

- Requests for upfront payments
- Requests for sensitive financial information
- Requests for identity documents without appropriate context
- Guaranteed or unusually high income
- Pressure to act immediately
- Requests to transfer money
- Unexpected checks or overpayments
- Unverified recruitment through messaging platforms

## Recommended Action

Do not share sensitive information or send money until the employer and opportunity have been independently verified.

A single warning sign does not automatically prove that a job is fraudulent. Multiple indicators should be considered together with the overall context.
