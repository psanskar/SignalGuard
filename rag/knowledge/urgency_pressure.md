# Urgency and Pressure Tactics in Job Scams

## Overview

Some fraudulent job postings use urgency or pressure to encourage applicants to act before they have enough time to verify the employer or opportunity.

## Common Warning Signs

Examples include:

- "Apply immediately"
- "Act now"
- "Limited positions available"
- "Only today"
- "Urgent hiring"
- Pressure to make a decision quickly
- Claims that an opportunity will disappear unless the applicant responds immediately

## Risk Indicator

Urgency alone does not prove that a job is fraudulent. However, pressure to act quickly can reduce an applicant's ability to independently verify the employer, recruiter, job details, or payment requirements.

Urgency becomes more concerning when combined with other warning signs such as:

- Upfront payment requests
- Recruitment through unofficial messaging platforms
- Unrealistic income promises
- Requests for sensitive personal or financial information
- Unverified employer identities

## Safety Guidance

Applicants should take time to verify the employer and job opportunity independently.

Do not allow claims of limited availability, immediate hiring, or urgent application deadlines to pressure you into sending money or sensitive information.

### Key Safety Recommendation

Take time to verify the employer, recruiter, job description, and any payment or information requests before proceeding.
