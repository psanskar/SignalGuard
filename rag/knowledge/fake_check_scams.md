## Risk Indicator

A recruiter or employer sending a check and asking the applicant to deposit it and then transfer money elsewhere can be a serious warning sign.

Common patterns include:

- Receiving an unexpected check
- Being asked to deposit a check
- Being told to purchase equipment using deposited funds
- Being asked to send part of the money back
- Being asked to transfer money after an apparent overpayment

## Safety Guidance

Do not assume that deposited funds are legitimate simply because they appear in a bank account.

## Recommended Action

Do not send money back or transfer funds based solely on instructions from an unverified recruiter.

Verify the employer and payment independently before taking action.
