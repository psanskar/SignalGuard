Job scammers may attempt to obtain sensitive financial information from applicants.

A recruiter or supposed employer may request:

- Bank account details
- Bank account numbers
- Banking information
- Debit card information
- Credit card information
- Online banking credentials
- Payment account information
- Other financial credentials

A request for financial information during recruitment should be treated cautiously, especially when the employer has not been independently verified.

Financial information can potentially be misused for unauthorized transactions or identity-related fraud.

## Safety Guidance

Do not provide banking credentials, card information, passwords, PINs, or other sensitive financial information to an unverified recruiter.

Independently verify the employer before providing any financial information.

Do not assume that a person is legitimate simply because they claim to represent a company.

A request for financial information does not automatically prove that a job is fraudulent. Consider the context and other warning signs in the job posting.
