Cryptocurrency can be used in fraudulent job and recruitment schemes.

A recruiter or supposed employer may ask a job applicant to:

- Pay a recruitment fee using Bitcoin
- Send money using cryptocurrency
- Transfer Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, or another cryptocurrency
- Send funds to a cryptocurrency wallet
- Purchase cryptocurrency as part of a supposed job requirement
- Deposit cryptocurrency before receiving employment or earnings
- Pay for equipment, training, or registration using cryptocurrency

A request for cryptocurrency during recruitment is a strong warning sign, particularly when the recruiter is unknown or the employer cannot be independently verified.

Cryptocurrency transactions may be difficult or impossible to reverse once sent.

## Safety Guidance

Do not send cryptocurrency to a recruiter or employer simply because they claim it is required for employment.

Do not transfer cryptocurrency to an unknown wallet address.

Do not purchase cryptocurrency to pay a recruitment, training, registration, or equipment fee without independently verifying the employer.

Verify the company and recruiter using contact information obtained independently from the job advertisement or recruiter.

A cryptocurrency-related request does not automatically prove that a job is fraudulent, but it should receive additional scrutiny, especially when combined with other warning signs such as guaranteed income, urgency, requests for personal information, or upfront payments.

## Common Cryptocurrency Job Scam Scenarios

Common cryptocurrency-related recruitment scams include:

- Asking applicants to send Bitcoin or BTC to a recruiter
- Asking applicants to buy Bitcoin before starting a job
- Requesting USDT or other cryptocurrency as a job-related payment
- Asking applicants to transfer cryptocurrency to a wallet address
- Requiring cryptocurrency payments before employment begins
- Asking applicants to purchase cryptocurrency as part of a job requirement

These requests should be treated as significant warning signs and independently verified before any money is transferred.
