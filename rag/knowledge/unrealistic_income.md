# Unrealistic Income Claims in Job Scams

## Overview

Fraudulent job postings may advertise unusually high, guaranteed, or easy earnings that are disproportionate to the work being described.

## Common Warning Signs

Examples include:

- "Guaranteed income"
- "Guaranteed earnings"
- "Guaranteed salary"
- "Earn ₹50,000 per week"
- "Make thousands of dollars per month"
- "Easy money"
- "Get rich quickly"
- Very high earnings for minimal hours or little experience
- Claims of guaranteed financial returns from ordinary entry-level work

## Risk Indicator

An unusually high or guaranteed income claim does not by itself prove that a job is fraudulent.

However, these claims deserve additional verification, especially when the posting also:

- Requires little or no experience
- Promises high earnings for only a few hours of work
- Requests upfront payments
- Uses external messaging platforms for recruitment
- Creates pressure to apply immediately
- Avoids providing verifiable employer information

## Safety Guidance

Applicants should compare advertised compensation with realistic market rates for the role and location.

Verify the employer, position, responsibilities, compensation structure, and payment terms through independently obtained sources.

Be especially cautious when guaranteed income is combined with requests for money or sensitive information.

### Key Safety Recommendation

Do not rely solely on unusually high or guaranteed earnings claims. Verify the employer and compensation details independently before accepting the opportunity.
