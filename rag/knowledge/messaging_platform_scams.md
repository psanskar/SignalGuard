## Risk Indicator

Recruitment conducted exclusively or unexpectedly through messaging platforms such as Telegram or WhatsApp can require additional verification.

## Safety Guidance

The use of a messaging platform alone does not prove that a job is fraudulent.

However, caution is appropriate when messaging-platform recruitment is combined with other warning signs such as:

- Requests for money
- Requests for sensitive information
- Guaranteed income
- Urgency or pressure
- Unverified employer identity

## Recommended Action

Verify the employer through an independently obtained website or contact channel.

Do not rely solely on information provided by the recruiter.
